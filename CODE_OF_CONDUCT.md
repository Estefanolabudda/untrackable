# Code of Conduct

Untrackable follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep
discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language and publishing other people's private information are not accepted
– in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space.

Never post personal data in public: no real sign-in, payment or e-mail links,
tokens, or URLs that identify you or someone else. Replace them with
placeholders such as `https://example.com/…`.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project contact,
**untrackable@estefano.me**. Security problems are
reported as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in public issues.

The maintainer reviews every report, keeps the reporter's identity
confidential and may remove contributions or block participants who violate
this code.
